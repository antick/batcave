Every good workshop needs someone who can look at an ambitious sketch and ask where the battery is supposed to go. At Wayne Enterprises, that person is Lucius.

## Explain the actual problem

My first sketch often includes wings. His first question is usually whether the job needs a ladder. This is an excellent, if personally inconvenient, design process.

In our fictional review sessions, we begin with the person using the tool, the task they need to finish, and the smallest reliable way to help them.

## Save the clever part for later

A tool that works on a rainy Tuesday is more useful than a spectacular prototype that works once. Lucius says this also applies to the car.

I have put the wings in a separate folder. He has named the folder “Later, Bruce.”
