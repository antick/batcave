The Batcomputer can cross-reference a city map with a decade of case notes. Yesterday it spent eleven minutes asking whether I wanted to restart now or remind myself later.

## The midnight update window

Lucius proposed a maintenance window. I explained that Gotham has never respected a maintenance window. We compromised: updates happen while Alfred makes tea.

The fictional system now saves a checkpoint before every upgrade. Apparently “I thought it would be fine” is not a backup strategy.

## One dashboard is enough

I used to keep a separate panel for every task. Now the [Batcomputer](/projects/batcomputer) starts with three things: what needs attention, what changed, and whether dinner is getting cold.

The third alert has the highest priority. Alfred configured that himself.
