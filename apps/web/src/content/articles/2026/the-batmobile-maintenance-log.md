The Batmobile has an excellent engine, very good tires, and the maintenance needs of a small airport.

## Write it down before the next drive

The fictional [Batmobile Log](/projects/batmobile-log) records every service, part replacement, and unusual noise. “Probably nothing” is not an accepted category anymore.

Lucius added a field for test results. Alfred added one for whether I remembered to bring the vehicle home with all its doors.

## Small checks prevent long evenings

A quick inspection beats a dramatic repair. Charge the batteries. Check the lights. Put the tools back where they belong.

I have also filed a feature request for a cup holder. This has become a surprisingly long discussion.
