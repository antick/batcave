From above, Gotham looks like a collection of shortcuts. At street level, it is a collection of people. It helps to remember which one matters more.

## The quiet parts of the city

In this fictional field note, I pass a bakery opening early, a library closing late, and a bus driver who waits for someone running down the block.

None of those people has a signal in the sky. All of them make the city easier to live in.

## A map should tell more than distance

The [Gotham Atlas](/projects/gotham-atlas) includes community spaces and places to get help, not just fast routes across town.

Alfred has added a second layer for coffee. He describes it as essential infrastructure. I have decided not to argue.
