A utility belt is a very small desk that follows you onto rooftops. If the important thing is in the wrong pocket, the whole design has failed.

## Every pocket earns its place

The imaginary [Utility Belt project](/projects/utility-belt) starts with a simple rule: frequently used tools should be easy to find without looking. Spare batteries get a sensible pocket. Novelty gadgets go back to the workshop.

| Pocket | Contents | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front | Grapnel controls | Easy to reach |
| Side | Spare power cell | Easy to replace |
| Hidden | Alfred’s snack | Non-negotiable |

## Test it with gloves on

Lucius asks me to try every latch while wearing the actual gloves. This has ended several very elegant designs before they became very expensive mistakes.

The best version is usually the one I can explain without a diagram. Except the snack pocket. That location is classified.
