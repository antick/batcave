The cape gets most of the attention. A working streetlight rarely makes the front page. I still think the streetlight deserves a better publicist.

## Start with the ordinary things

At the Wayne Foundation, my favorite fictional project board is the least dramatic one: repair a library roof, reopen a community room, give a neighborhood somewhere warm to gather.

There is no grand entrance. Someone turns a key, the lights come on, and the place becomes useful again.

## Listen before drawing the blueprint

A neighborhood usually knows what it needs. My job is to show up, listen, and make the budget behave. Alfred says that last part also applies to the garage.

The [Wayne Foundation project](/projects/wayne-foundation) is an imaginary example of how I would keep those small promises visible. Gotham needs more than one person on a rooftop. It needs people who can get a library open on Monday.
