I have a foundation, a research division, and an unusually complicated garage. Alfred has a notebook. His system works better.

## The list is short for a reason

His imaginary morning checklist contains three items: eat something, answer the important message, and finish the thing already on the workbench.

Mine used to contain forty-seven. Several were just the words “improve grapnel” written with increasing urgency.

## Leave room for the unexpected

Alfred leaves space between appointments because he understands Gotham. A meeting runs late. A neighbor needs help. Someone parks an experimental vehicle in the wrong entrance.

I am building [a calendar](/projects/alfred-calendar) around that idea. He has already requested administrator access.
