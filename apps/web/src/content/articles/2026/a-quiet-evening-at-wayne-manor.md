There is a particular kind of silence at Wayne Manor after the last car leaves. Alfred puts the kettle on. I attempt to sit in a chair like someone who has done it before.

## Put the case file down

In this fictional evening, nothing urgent happens for twenty whole minutes. I read a chapter. The tea stays warm. The Batcomputer is not invited.

Alfred calls this a routine. I am still treating it as a promising prototype.

## Rest belongs on the calendar

A clear head is useful in a board meeting and on a rooftop. So is remembering the people who make the manor feel like a home.

Tomorrow can have its puzzles. Tonight, I am letting Alfred choose the film.
